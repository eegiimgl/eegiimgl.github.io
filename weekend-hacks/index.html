Projects & Experiments

In my free time, I build tools to solve my own problems and then explore how they can help others. These are mostly fun, experimental projects, so my focus is on the solution design rather than building a flawless product. I strongly believe the most powerful solutions are simple ones. If you have feedback, want to collaborate, or have a problem you need help solving, feel free to reach out!
